### Product Direction Decision

- Treat this as a policy-document-led B2B product, not an admin CRUD application.
- Platform admins should operate the platform and govern access; they should not be responsible for creating every employer or provider organization.
- Employer HR teams should be the primary customer and self-register or join through an invitation/onboarding workflow.
- Insurers/providers should usually be onboarded by the platform, broker, or employer implementation team; a provider portal can be optional for invited partners.
- Plan records should be derived from provider documents instead of requiring admins to manually enter every plan field.
- Use a normalized canonical schema for comparison-critical terms, while preserving raw document wording and source citations because providers can express equivalent terms differently.
- Support source provenance, confidence, unmapped terms, effective dates, and document/version history in the future normalized policy model.
- The current rigid plan editor and platform-admin organization form are transitional prototype workflows and should not define the final product.

### Plan-Version Responsibility Correction

- HR should not manually author normalized insurance terms or certify that policy terms are correct.
- HR's role is to provide source material they received from a carrier, broker, or TPA, such as a renewal booklet, quote, benefit summary, or rate sheet.
- The platform should create a draft version from the imported source, extract normalized fields, and retain the original wording and citations.
- A carrier/provider reviewer should approve the extracted version when available.
- If no provider reviewer is available, a trained broker/platform operator can review it, with the approval actor and confidence clearly recorded.
- Therefore, the current employer `POST /plans/{plan_id}/versions` behavior should evolve from "HR submits terms" to "HR imports source material and the platform creates a draft version."

### Product Viability Assessment
