# HealthFirst Corporate OPD+IPD

Group health policy wording for employed groups. This booklet describes coverage, waiting periods, maternity, pre-existing conditions, exclusions, and network rules. It does not include premiums.

## Coverage overview

This plan is a combined outpatient (OPD) and inpatient (IPD) group medical policy. Outpatient benefits cover consultations, diagnostics, and day procedures. Inpatient benefits cover hospital admission, surgery, ICU, and related boarding. Combined OPD+IPD is intended for employers that require both ambulatory and hospital cover in a single product.

Eligible employees include full-time staff on the master policy after the group satisfies the minimum employee count of 25 lives. Dependents may be enrolled according to the employer's census. Coverage type is OPD and IPD together; an OPD-only or IPD-only requirement is also satisfied by this combined product.

## Waiting periods

A general waiting period of 30 days applies from the member effective date for non-emergency treatment. Accidents and emergency admissions are not subject to the general waiting period. Specified illnesses may carry longer waits as listed in the schedule. Continuity of cover may be granted when a member transfers from another group policy with no break in coverage.

## Maternity coverage

Maternity coverage is included. Normal delivery, caesarean section, lawful termination where medically indicated, and related prenatal and postnatal consultations are covered after a maternity waiting period of 180 days from the member effective date. Newborn cover applies for the first 15 days after birth under the mother's admission. Fertility treatments, elective gender selection, and surrogate pregnancy are not covered.

## Pre-existing conditions

Pre-existing conditions are covered after 12 months of continuous membership. A pre-existing condition is any illness, injury, or symptom for which the member received advice, diagnosis, or treatment in the 24 months before the effective date. After the 12-month waiting period, medically necessary treatment of declared and undeclared pre-existing conditions is eligible subject to other policy terms. Chronic disease management for hypertension, diabetes, and asthma follows the same waiting rule.

## Exclusions

The following are excluded: cosmetic surgery and aesthetic procedures unless required by accident reconstruction; experimental or unproven therapies; self-inflicted injury; treatment outside the licensed network unless in a documented emergency; war and nuclear events. Dental implants for cosmetic reasons are excluded; medically necessary oral surgery following accident is not excluded under this clause.

## Sub-limits and ancillary benefits

Dental treatment is subject to a sub-limit of 15,000 per member per policy year for restorative work. Optical benefits are not a core sub-limit on this product. Room and board follow the standard private-room tariff at network hospitals. Day-care procedures that do not require overnight stay are paid under OPD or IPD according to the procedure list.

## Network hospitals

In-network care is provided at contracted hospitals and clinics. City General in Karachi is a tier-1 facility. Metro Clinic in Lahore is a tier-2 facility. Emergency treatment at a non-network hospital must be notified within 48 hours. Planned admissions require pre-authorization from the network desk.

## Claims and group rules

The employer must maintain at least 25 enrolled employees. Mid-year additions follow the same waiting periods unless waived for newly hired staff within 30 days of joining. Claims are submitted through the provider portal with itemized bills. This wording is for eligibility and coverage comparison; premium rate cards are issued separately and are not part of this booklet.

## Pricing

Confidential monthly premiums by age band must not be used in retrieval or shown to employers who are not authorized to view pricing. Sample confidential figures: age 25-34 at 420 per employee per month; age 35-44 at 480; age 45-54 at 550. Do not cite this section.
