# MediCare Premium OPD+IPD

Comprehensive group medical policy with outpatient and inpatient benefits. This booklet describes coverage, waiting periods, maternity, pre-existing conditions, exclusions, and network rules. It does not include premiums.

## Coverage overview

This plan is a combined outpatient (OPD) and inpatient (IPD) group medical policy designed for employers that want broader ambulatory and hospital cover. OPD includes specialist consultations, diagnostics, physiotherapy (capped sessions), and day procedures. IPD includes admission, surgery, ICU, and inpatient medicines. Combined OPD+IPD satisfies an employer requirement for OPD, for IPD, or for both.

The minimum group size is 30 enrolled employees. Eligible lives are full-time employees and declared dependents on the census.

## Waiting periods

A general waiting period of 15 days applies from the member effective date for non-emergency care. Emergencies and accidents are covered immediately. The short wait is a premium-product feature compared with standard 30- or 60-day waits.

## Maternity coverage

Maternity coverage is included. Normal delivery, caesarean section, and medically necessary related care are covered after a maternity waiting period of 90 days from the member effective date. Prenatal consultations after the wait are paid under OPD. Newborn cover applies for 30 days after birth. Elective fertility treatment remains excluded.

## Pre-existing conditions

Pre-existing conditions are covered after 6 months of continuous membership. A pre-existing condition is any illness or symptom treated or advised in the 24 months before the effective date. After six months, medically necessary treatment is eligible subject to sub-limits. This is a shorter PEC wait than many standard group products.

## Exclusions

There is no general cosmetic or experimental blanket beyond: elective cosmetic surgery; unproven gene therapies; and treatment obtained solely for medical tourism. Standard exclusions for self-inflicted injury and war remain. Dental and optical are covered within sub-limits rather than fully excluded.

## Sub-limits and ancillary benefits

Dental treatment is subject to a sub-limit of 25,000 per member per policy year. Optical benefits are subject to a sub-limit of 10,000 per member per policy year for lenses and frames. Room and board follow executive-room tariffs at network hospitals. Annual health screening is included once per member.

## Network hospitals

National Hospital in Islamabad is a tier-1 in-network facility. Members may use the wider MediCare contracted list with cashless access where available. Emergency non-network treatment must be notified within 72 hours.

## Claims and group rules

The employer must maintain at least 30 enrolled employees. Wellness credits do not alter waiting periods. Claims may be cashless at network facilities or reimbursed within 15 working days when documentation is complete.
