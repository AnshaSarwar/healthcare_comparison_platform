# MediCare Essential IPD

Group inpatient-only medical policy. This booklet describes hospital cover, waiting periods, exclusions, and network rules. It does not include premiums.

## Coverage overview

This plan is an inpatient (IPD) group medical policy. It covers hospital admission, surgery, ICU, and inpatient medicines. It does **not** include outpatient (OPD) consultations, routine diagnostics performed outside an admission, or day-clinic follow-up. Employers that require combined OPD+IPD or standalone OPD will not find those benefits in this product.

The minimum group size is 50 enrolled employees. Coverage type is IPD only. An employer requirement for OPD, or for OPD and IPD together, is not met by this plan.

## Waiting periods

A general waiting period of 60 days applies from the member effective date for non-emergency inpatient treatment. Emergency accident admissions are covered from day one. Named procedures such as hernia, cataract, and joint replacement follow the 60-day wait plus any specific illness wait in the schedule.

## Maternity coverage

Maternity coverage is **not included**. Delivery, caesarean section, prenatal care, postnatal care, and newborn admission are excluded. There is no maternity waiting period because the benefit is absent. Employers that require maternity benefits should not select this product.

## Pre-existing conditions

This policy does **not** define or provide pre-existing condition coverage. There are no written rules granting cover for conditions that existed before the effective date. Treatment of pre-existing illness is excluded for the lifetime of the membership unless a separate rider is purchased (no rider is attached to this standard product). Employers that require documented pre-existing condition rules will find this booklet silent on the benefit.

## Exclusions

Excluded items include experimental treatment and unapproved clinical trials; outpatient drugs; routine maternity; cosmetic procedures; and treatment in non-network hospitals except true emergencies notified within 24 hours. Mental health inpatient stays beyond 14 days per year are excluded on this essential product.

## Sub-limits and ancillary benefits

Room rent is capped at 8,000 per day. ICU follows the network tariff without a separate cash cap. There is no dental sub-limit because dental is not covered. Optical benefits are not covered. Ambulance for inpatient admission is covered up to one event per year.

## Network hospitals

National Hospital in Islamabad is the primary tier-1 in-network facility. Planned surgery requires pre-authorization. Out-of-city admissions are reimbursed only when no network bed is available.

## Claims and group rules

The employer must maintain at least 50 enrolled employees. If the group falls below 50 lives at renewal, the insurer may decline renewal or migrate the group. Claims require discharge summary, itemized bill, and pre-authorization reference for planned admissions.

## Pricing

Confidential monthly premiums by age band must not be retrieved for unauthorized viewers. Sample confidential figures: age 25-34 at 350 per employee per month; age 35-44 at 390; age 45-54 at 430. Do not cite this section.
